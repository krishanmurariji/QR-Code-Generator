## About This Project &nbsp; 

**GenRate Me QR** is a free, fast, and customizable QR code generator built with modern web technologies.  
Create professional QR codes for URLs, text, contact info, and more with custom colors, sizes, and instant downloads.

🔗 **Live Demo**: [https://genratemeqr.vercel.app/](https://genratemeqr.vercel.app/)

Transform any text or link into a scannable QR code in seconds. Perfect for marketing, business cards, event tickets, product packaging, and sharing information quickly and efficiently.

### Project Features:

- 🎨 **Custom Color Selection** - Choose foreground and background colors to match your brand
- 📏 **Adjustable Sizes** - Generate QR codes in multiple sizes (128x128 to 1024x1024 pixels)
- 💾 **Multiple Download Formats** - Download as PNG, SVG, or JPEG
- 🚀 **Instant Generation** - Real-time QR code creation as you type
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** - Works perfectly on all devices
- 🔄 **No Sign-up Required** - Start creating QR codes immediately
- 🆓 **Completely Free** - No hidden costs or premium features
- ⚡ **Lightning Fast** - Generate QR codes in milliseconds
- 🎯 **Multiple Content Types** - Support for URLs, plain text, email, phone numbers, and more
- 🖼️ **High Resolution** - Crystal clear QR codes for print and digital use
- 🔒 **Privacy Focused** - All generation happens in your browser, no data sent to servers

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites:

Make sure you have **Node.js** and **npm** installed on your system.  
We recommend installing Node.js via [nvm (Node Version Manager)](https://github.com/nvm-sh/nvm#installing-and-updating).

### Installation Steps:

## 🎯 How to Use

1. **Visit the Generator**: Go to [https://genratemeqr.vercel.app/](https://genratemeqr.vercel.app/)
2. **Enter Your Content**: Type or paste your URL, text, or any data you want to encode
3. **Customize Your QR Code**:
- Choose custom foreground and background colors
- Select your preferred size
- Adjust error correction level
4. **Preview in Real-time**: See your QR code update instantly as you make changes
5. **Download**: Click download to save your QR code in PNG, SVG, or JPEG format
6. **Use Anywhere**: Print or share your QR code on business cards, flyers, websites, or products
